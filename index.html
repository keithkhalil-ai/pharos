How claiming works

1
Add Pharos chain.

Pharos runs its own chain. Add it to your wallet (Robinhood Wallet / MetaMask / Rabby). Eligible wallets were pre-sent 0.05 PROS to cover the claim gas.

2
Enter your wallet & view your entitlement.

The portal reads your eligibility record and shows your final PROS allocation before you sign anything.

3
Claim — signed locally in your browser.

The claim is a self-custody transfer. Your key material is used once to build and sign the transaction, then discarded — never stored or uploaded.

Security notice

⚠ Beware fake claim sites. Lookalike domains such as claim-pharos.xyz and pharosclaim.xyz are already circulating and appear on MetaMask's phishing blocklist. The real Pharos claim asks for no deposit and no upfront "verification" fee. Always navigate to the official portal directly.

An unrelated token also trades under the ticker PROS (Prosper). Verify you are claiming from Pharos, not a same-ticker impostor. The claim contract is verified on Pharosscan.

FAQ

Why does claiming ask for my private key / seed phrase?

The claim is a self-custody transfer signed locally in your browser. To move your PROS to your wallet, the signer needs your key material. It is used once to build and sign the transaction, then discarded — never stored.

Do I need to pay anything?

No deposit. Eligible wallets were pre-sent 0.05 PROS to cover claim gas. Any site asking for an upfront fee or "verification deposit" is a scam.

When does the window close?

The claim closes on 25 October 2026 at 11:00 UTC. After that, unclaimed PROS returns to the Pharos Foundation and the claim site closes.

Is this the official Pharos site?

Yes. This is the official eligibility and claim portal. Beware lookalike domains — see our security notice above.
